Privacy Policy

AI Social Media Content Engine
Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Social Media Content Engine handles information when it connects to Pinterest and other services used by the automation workflow.

1. About the Application

AI Social Media Content Engine is a personal automation project built with n8n. It takes content ideas, uses AI to generate social media content and image prompts, generates visual content, and can publish content to connected social media accounts.

2. Information We Access

When the application is connected to Pinterest, it may access information necessary to perform the requested Pinterest actions, such as the authenticated account's boards and Pins and information required to create Pins.

The application is intended for personal use. It does not intentionally access or collect other Pinterest users' private information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected services is used only to:

  • Authenticate the connected account.
  • Identify the user's available boards when required.
  • Create and publish Pins requested by the user.
  • Operate and troubleshoot the personal automation workflow.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information obtained through Pinterest is not sold to third parties or used for advertising purposes.

Content may be sent to the third-party services explicitly used by the workflow, such as AI or image-generation services, only when necessary to perform the requested automation.

5. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are handled through the automation environment and are not intentionally made public. Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and connected account information.

No security system can guarantee absolute security, so users should never publicly share API keys, access tokens, client secrets, or other authentication credentials.

6. Data Retention

The application does not intentionally maintain a separate database of Pinterest users' personal information. Information may remain in the connected automation environment only for as long as necessary to operate the workflow or maintain its execution records.

7. Your Choices

You can disconnect the application from your Pinterest account or revoke its authorization through the relevant Pinterest account and application settings. Because this is a personal-use automation, access can also be removed by disabling or deleting the corresponding credentials in the automation environment.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is not directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
